# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students, both school-age teens and university students, studying on their own from their own class material. They use Novalern on a phone and on a laptop in roughly equal measure (user confirmed), usually in short sessions: revising notes after class, getting unstuck on a homework problem, preparing for a test.

## Product Purpose

Novalern turns a student's own notes, textbook pages, photos, PDFs or typed questions into a short narrated video lesson, then keeps the knowledge alive with a daily exam and a weekly recap built from exactly what they studied. Success is a student who understood something today and can still answer questions on it at the end of the week.

## Positioning

Other tools either explain (chatbots, text answers) or test (flashcards, quizzes). Novalern does both from the student's own material, and its explanation is a video assembled live from structured scenes: narration, headline, key points, a real picture or diagram, a sticker, and voice, rather than a wall of text or a pre-rendered file. Exams can be answered by multiple choice, typing, or speaking, with AI grading and feedback.

## Operating Context

- Entry points: Home, Learn with AI, Daily exam, Weekly recap, Profile.
- A "lesson" is one Learn with AI session (a question or upload plus follow-ups), saved with an auto-generated title, topic and summary. History lists lessons.
- Daily exam: pick lessons, mode (multiple choice / written / voice), difficulty, length; results with per-question feedback; streak and averages. Weekly recap: lessons of the last 7 days grouped by topic, then a recap exam.
- Voice input and narration depend on the browser (Chrome and Edge for speech recognition); narration falls back from Groq text-to-speech to the browser voice.
- Deployed on Vercel at https://novalern-sigma.vercel.app; Firebase Auth and Firestore; Groq models; Giphy stickers; Wikimedia Commons / Wikipedia pictures; Freesound effects.
- Submitted to a Devpost hackathon; a demo video will be recorded from the live app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sign in with email/password or Google. Each user sees only their own lessons and exams.
- Uploads: PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF up to 6 MB; up to 3 images per message reach the vision model.
- Lesson scripts have 4 to 16 scenes; narration is capped at 200 characters per scene for the voice API.
- Visual lookups (stickers, pictures, sounds) are best-effort and may be missing; the lesson must still play.
- Undecided: pricing, teacher accounts, sharing lessons, spaced repetition (all listed as future work, none built).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Novalern, wordmark "novalern." with "nova" in dark navy, "lern" in the logo green, and a green dot. Icon: white "n." on dark navy with a lime-green dot (`public/icon.png`, `public/logo.png`).
- Colours the user pinned for the app: off-white ground, the logo green (#5BA300 family) as the accent, dark navy ink for text.
- Typography the user pinned: a pixel-style face for headings, the greeting and the video player; a clean readable face elsewhere.
- Tone: direct, friendly, not hype. "Learn it today. Remember it all week."
- Layout the user pinned: minimal and professional; no boxed cards for lists (History and its lessons are open, separated by rules, with the heading underlined); the "Hi user" greeting is plain text with no coloured block; Learn with AI is the main action; the Learn with AI page is just a slogan and the input bar; the video player adapts to any viewport with no inner scrollbar.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

1. The student's own material is the source of everything; nothing generic.
2. Teach by showing and telling in small steps, never by a wall of text.
3. Every lesson leads to a test; remembering is the goal, not reading.
4. Phone and laptop are equals; nothing may depend on a wide screen.
5. External services may fail; the core loop must still work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Voice input and spoken narration serve students who find reading hard; every spoken thing also has a transcript, and every exam can be taken by typing.
